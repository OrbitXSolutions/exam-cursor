  audit, organization/settings, and notifications.

  use filesystem locations. Background services expire attempts, deliver
  notifications, clean logs/media, and check licenses.
- Logging is intended to combine Serilog, database audit/action records, and a
  channel-backed SystemLogs subsystem. The latter is not included in this clone.
