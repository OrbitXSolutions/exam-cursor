ACCEPTANCE BOUNDARY
Even a backend-only workload with 50 independent candidates is not evidence of
50 camera-enabled live browser examinations. Production acceptance must include:
- Two real application nodes behind the customer's balancer, split SignalR peers,
  actual shared storage/key ring, customer PKI, and configured Redis backplane.
- Representative exam sizes and timings, answer/autosave/heartbeat traffic,
  proctor viewers, camera/screen monitoring and media uploads as configured.
- Synchronized start/submission bursts, reconnection, node loss/restart and
  inspection of persisted answers, terminal attempt states and events.
- Latency/error/rejection rates, CPU/GC/memory, SQL waits/pools, storage/network
  throughput, logging loss/queue pressure and notification delivery outcomes.
- Real inbox/handset receipt separately from provider acceptance and separately
  from the durability of queued notification records.
Thresholds and permitted degradation must be agreed with the owner, not invented.
